# Privacy Policy - HydroFuel Smart Trip Cost

**Last Updated:** October 2026

**HydroFuel: Smart Trip Cost** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
HydroFuel is designed with user privacy in mind. We do not require users to register an account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

### 2. Local Data Storage
All data entered into the application—including trip calculations, vehicle details, custom preferences, and saved logs—is stored locally on your device using local storage mechanisms. This data is not shared with or transferred to any external server.

### 3. Third-Party Services
Our application does not integrate third-party tracking, analytics SDKs, or data collection tools that monitor your activity across other apps or websites.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates are effective immediately upon posting to this page.
